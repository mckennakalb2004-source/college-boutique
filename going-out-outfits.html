Cute College Going-Out Outfits

Find outfit inspiration for dinner with friends, birthday celebrations and nights out. College Closet makes it easy to put together fun looks using versatile pieces and affordable style ideas.

Find Your Next Night-Out Look

Getting ready to go out can be one of the fun parts of college life. Whether you prefer a simple outfit or like dressing up, start with pieces that make you feel comfortable and confident.

Cute going-out outfits for college girls do not have to be expensive. A favorite pair of jeans, a statement top and a few accessories can create a look that works for different plans. Dresses, skirts and layering pieces also offer plenty of ways to change up your style.

Three Affordable Outfit Ideas

Dinner With Friends

Pair dark jeans with a fitted top, simple jewelry and ankle boots or flats. Add a jacket for a look that can go from dinner to dessert.

Birthday Celebration

Try a simple dress with a small shoulder bag and comfortable shoes. A fun accessory can add color without making the outfit feel overdone.

Casual Night Out

Style wide-leg denim with a going-out top and your favorite sneakers or boots. A belt and coordinated accessories can bring the look together.

How to Shop for Going-Out Clothes on a Budget

Before buying something new, think about how often you could wear it and what you could pair it with. Choosing versatile tops, denim and accessories makes it easier to create several outfits from fewer items.

You can also restyle clothes you already own by changing your shoes, bag or jewelry. Planning a few outfit combinations before shopping can help you avoid unnecessary purchases and stick to your budget.

Quick Styling Tip

Pick your main outfit piece first, then build around it. If your top or dress has a bold color or pattern, keep your accessories simple. If your outfit is neutral, use accessories to add a pop of color.
