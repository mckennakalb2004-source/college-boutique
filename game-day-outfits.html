College Game-Day Outfits

Football Saturdays are a chance to show your school spirit and have fun with your style. Find college game-day outfit inspiration that combines school colors, comfortable pieces and accessories for a full day with friends.

Plan Your Football Saturday Look

A great game-day outfit can start with one school-color piece and a few wardrobe basics. Try a sweatshirt with denim, a team-color top with jeans or a skirt with comfortable boots. Choose pieces that fit the weather and your plans before and after the game.

If you're searching for Iowa game-day outfits, black and gold can be an easy color combination to build around. Add a jacket for colder games or a simple accessory to make your look feel more personal.

Three Game-Day Outfit Ideas

Classic Casual Fan

Pair a school-color sweatshirt with straight-leg jeans and sneakers. This relaxed outfit works well for walking around campus and spending time with friends.

Denim and School Colors

Style a school-color tank or tee with denim shorts or jeans. Add a belt, sunglasses and a light layer when the weather changes.

Cool-Weather Game Day

Layer a long-sleeve top under a jacket with dark denim and boots. A scarf or beanie can add warmth while keeping the outfit coordinated.

Game-Day Style Tip

Check the weather before choosing your outfit. Comfortable shoes and layers you can remove or add will help you enjoy the day from pregame plans through the final whistle.

Make Your Outfit Your Own

You do not need a brand-new wardrobe for every game. Rewear your favorite jeans, switch up your top and add accessories to create a different look. Planning around pieces you already own can keep your game-day style affordable.
